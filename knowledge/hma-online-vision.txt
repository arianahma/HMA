01 THE CREATIVE DIRECTION

Build an immersive, nature-led digital sanctuary, not a conventional course dashboard. The experience should feel

elevated, deeply calm, intelligent, emotionally warm and effortless. “Heaven” is an aesthetic metaphor for beauty,

openness, renewal and possibility—not a religious claim or a promise of healing.

Visual motifs: forest canopy, living leaves, soft wind, birds, water ripples, sunlight, warm firelight, natural stone,

distant horizons, clouds, stars and moonlight. Use restrained cinematic motion and generous negative space. Avoid

fantasy overload, glitter, constant animations, stock-coaching clichés and crowded screens.

04 TIME-AWARE LIVING ENVIRONMENTS

(cid:127) Dawn (local time): mist, rising amber light, birdsong, dew and gentle green movement.

(cid:127) Day: sunlit trees, warm stone, clear water, fresh green foliage and a subtle breeze.

(cid:127) Dusk: bronze light, a glowing horizon, softer birds and reflective water.

Offer “Auto / Day / Night” controls and a static low-motion alternative. Time-of-day is an atmosphere setting, never a

barrier to using any feature.

05 PERSONALISED AESTHETIC WITHOUT STEREOTYPES

Offer Botanical Light (ivory, sage, warm gold, softer organic textures), Architectural Forest (deep forest, charcoal

stone, bronze, strong geometry) and Balanced Nature (neutral green and stone). These may be described as softer,

more masculine or neutral moods, but any Architect can choose any theme. Content, features and outcomes remain

identical.

06 SOUND DESIGN & BINAURAL AUDIO

(cid:127) Audio starts off by default. Offer separate toggles for nature ambience, music and guided voice, with volume controls

and mute always visible.

(cid:127) Nature layers: birds, breeze, water, fire crackle, rain and nocturnal insects; mix gently and avoid startling loops.

(cid:127) Optional binaural tracks require headphones for the intended stereo effect. Present as a listening preference, not a

proven treatment. Provide a normal ambient alternative.

(cid:127) Respect device mute, reduced motion, bandwidth and battery. Audio must stop when requested and never

unexpectedly restart.

09 PERSONALISATION & JOURNEY

(cid:127) Private advisory tier: eight sessions in 28 days, with relevant levels unlocked after sessions; standalone tier

10 WISDOM, MEDITATION & RITUALS

Wisdom Cards: curated, accurately attributed selections from Bible/Christianity, Islam, Stoicism, Buddhism, Taoism,

Shaolin/Zen, Judaism, Hinduism and other chosen traditions. Themes: courage, anxiety, purpose, forgiveness, love,

gratitude and peace. Avoid misattributed quotes and religious blending that misrepresents sources.

Meditations: presence, abundance, relationships, confidence, performance, sleep, spiritual reflection, emotional

grounding and body awareness. Include breathwork for everyday stress, interviews, public speaking and first-date

nerves; avoid medical cure claims or unsafe breath-holding practices.

Morning Arrival: intention + breath + wisdom. Afternoon Reset: nourishment, hydration and presence. Evening Return:

gratitude, reflection and rest. Reminders are configurable and never guilt-inducing.

11 PROGRESS & FIRAS APP INTEGRATION

Track day completed, real-world actions, optional mood/energy/focus ratings and private reflections. With explicit

consent, integrate sleep, hydration, nutrition, activity and outdoor-light data from supported sources. Display trends

and individual baselines, not fabricated transformation scores or diagnostic claims.

Provide a phone-only mode; wearable integration is optional. Clearly explain data access, retention, export, deletion,

security and what the founder/advisor can see. Private journal entries should not be visible to others by default.

13 BUILD ORDER & ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

Phase 1 — MVP: brand system, lightweight nature arrival, day/night modes, login, 28-day ARCH™ curriculum, rituals,

consented tracker integration.

product experiences.

Definition of done: A new Architect can log in, select preferences, begin Day 1 within 30 seconds after setup,

complete the daily journey, return to real life, and later see meaningful progress—without audio surprises, forced

content or visual clutter.
