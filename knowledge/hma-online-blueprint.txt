HMA ONLINE | 28-DAY ARCH™ PROGRAMME

Product and content blueprint for Firas | 19 November 2026

One meaningful shift each day. A lifetime of conscious mastery.

Product principle: HMA should not demand more of an Architect’s attention. It should repeatedly return

their attention to their life.

Purpose: Translate the private 28-day ARCH™ framework into an elegant, accessible digital experience

that supports awareness, intentional choices, everyday action and measurable personal reflection.

Experience standards: Mobile-first; calm and uncluttered; warm, intelligent language; optional

reminders; clear consent and privacy controls; no engagement pressure or endless feeds.

01 HOME SCREEN & NAVIGATION

Greeting: Good morning, Architect. Adapt greeting to local time. Optional supporting line: Your life. Your

vision. Your Architecture.

• Morning Arrival - intention, breath and chosen wisdom

• Today’s Architecture - the current ARCH™ day and one next action

• Afternoon Reset - brief nourishment, movement and presence prompt

• Evening Return - reflection, gratitude and rest intention

• My Journal - private reflections and life-story entries

• Meditations - searchable guided practices

• Wisdom Cards - optional spiritual/philosophical traditions

• My Architecture / Progress - journey, check-ins and personal insights

Navigation rule: One primary action on the home screen at a time. All other areas remain available

without requiring completion.

03 DAILY LEARNING TEMPLATE

• Discover: one 10–15-minute video or audio lesson

• Reflect: one meaningful exercise or journal prompt

• Act: one real-world action, achievable that day

• Observe: one optional tracker check-in and short reflection

Estimated core time: 15–20 minutes, with flexible pacing. Include an optional meditation, relevant

journal entry and wellbeing data insight where useful. Do not turn the daily journey into a long

checklist.

Day screen fields: day number; ARCH™ phase; one-sentence outcome; lesson media; exercise; action;

optional meditation; optional data insight; complete / revisit controls.

04 IMMERSIVE EXPERIENCE

Life Vision Immersion

A cinematic welcome experience exploring the Architect’s life journey, from beginnings to the legacy

they hope to create. A personal vision film may be replayed morning, afternoon or evening by choice.

Meditation Sanctuary

Guided meditations aligned with personal pillars; optional ambient or binaural audio with a persistent

on/off setting. No claims that audio frequencies treat medical conditions.

Life Story Journal

Optional prompts to recall a first or formative encounter with a life theme, what was felt then, what is

felt now and which meaning the Architect chooses today. Allow skipping, editing and private storage.

Vision Board and Life Story Book

Digital collection of aspirations, desired feelings, relationships, experiences, achievements and legacy.

Support export/print; physical book as a later offering.

05 PERSONAL PILLARS

During onboarding, invite each Architect to choose one primary pillar and optional secondary pillars.

Use a short baseline questionnaire, tailored reflection and suggested practices.

• Mind Mastery - patterns, beliefs, awareness and emotional regulation

• Clarity and Decisions - focus, discernment and purposeful action

• Love and Relationships - self-connection, family, parenting and intimacy

• Joy and Presence - gratitude, awe and being fully where you are

• Physical Vitality - movement, strength, nourishment and recovery

• Body and Emotional Balance - stress awareness and understanding body signals

• Courage and Resilience - navigating fear, guilt, pressure and uncertainty

• Spiritual Connection - faith, meaning and the Architect’s chosen worldview

• Freedom and Abundance - relationship with money, possibility and choice

• Leadership and Legacy - contribution, influence and human impact

Personalisation: Chosen pillar informs suggested wisdom, meditations, exercises, reminders and

progress reflections. The Architect can change their focus at any time.

06 DAILY RITUALS & NOTIFICATIONS

Morning Arrival

Default suggestion: 5–6 a.m., adjusted to the Architect’s chosen schedule. One intention, short

breathing invitation, optional wisdom card and vision reminder.

Afternoon Reset

Default: lunchtime. A brief reminder to eat mindfully, hydrate, move or return to the present moment.

Evening Return

Default: bedtime. Gratitude, brief journaling, relaxation and a gentle intention for tomorrow.

Notification controls: Opt-in by ritual; choose time, theme, frequency and quiet hours; pause all

reminders. No guilt-based streaks or alarming language.

08 MEDITATION & BREATHWORK LIBRARY

Browse by intention: abundance and vision; presence and joy; fear and anxiety; confidence for

interviews, public speaking and first dates; love and connection; spiritual connection; physical

relaxation; sleep and recovery; emotional balance.

Each practice: title, intention, duration, audio/video, optional soundscape, gentle safety note and

save/favourite. Practices support wellbeing and are not treatments or promises to eliminate pain,

depression, anxiety, hormonal changes or other medical conditions.

09 MY ARCHITECTURE / PROGRESS

Show the ARCH™ day and phase, completed exercises, chosen actions, journal reflections and

user-selected wellbeing trends. Compare personal baselines over time; avoid scores implying human

worth or guaranteed transformation.

Optional Firas app data: sleep, hydration, movement, nutrition, outdoor light, energy, mood, focus and

self-reported wellbeing. Support phone-only entry and compatible wearable integration where

technically feasible and permissioned.

Data principles: explicit consent, clear source labels, manual correction, data minimisation,

export/delete options and private-by-default journaling. No automatic advisor access to intimate journal

entries.

10 PRIVATE ADVISORY EXPERIENCE

Private HMA includes eight 90-minute sessions over 28 days (two per week), agreed between Architect

and advisor. Digital support may include personalised exercises, unlocked levels, session reflections,

weekly reviews, Personal Architecture™, Final Architecture and a 90-Day Strategic Direction.

The standalone digital programme remains a distinct self-guided option. Do not imply that self-guided

subscribers receive private sessions or direct advisory access.

11 HMA LIFESTYLE COLLECTION | FUTURE EXPANSION

Optional physical extensions: Life Story Books, printed journals, vision boards, bookmarks, HMA bags,

apparel and activewear, yoga mats, candles, essential oils, nutrition guidance and movement

programmes. Treat these as a future shop/content roadmap, not launch requirements.

13 IMPLEMENTATION & CONTENT RULES

• Maintain a single source of truth for day content, media, exercises, wisdom and meditation metadata.

• Separate content administration from the Architect-facing app; make content editable without code changes.

• Store per-user progress and preferences securely; support accessibility, captions and low-bandwidth audio.

• Clarify account ownership, administrator roles, hosting, backups, source-code repository, analytics and handover

access.

• Before launch: test onboarding, each day’s flow, reminders, private unlocks, consent, export/delete and recovery

paths.
